## Long description

### Your store now speaks on WhatsApp

Dowaba AI Connector links your Magento store to the **Dowaba** AI platform. Your customers message in natural language from their favorite channel — **WhatsApp, Instagram DM, email or phone** — and the AI answers with your store's live data.

```
Customer (WhatsApp): "Do you have the iPhone 15 Pro? How much?"
Dowaba AI:           "Yes ✅ $1,099, in stock. Color options..."
Customer:            "I'd like to order one"
Dowaba AI:           "Summary: iPhone 15 Pro × 1 = $1,099 + shipping. Confirm?"
Customer:            "Yes"
Dowaba AI:           "Order placed #000000123 🎉"
```

### What it does (10 AI capabilities)

- 🔍 **Product search & detail** — by name, SKU or keyword
- ⚖️ **Product comparison** — 2-3 products side by side with specs
- 📊 **Stock check** — real time
- 🗂️ **Category browsing**
- 📋 **Order tracking** — order number + email match (privacy-safe)
- 👤 **Customer recognition** — personalized replies with order history
- 🛒 **Cart recovery**
- ✅ **Order placement** — with customer confirmation, 2-step safe flow

### Why Dowaba?

- **24/7** — the 3 AM "is this in stock?" never goes unanswered
- **30+ languages** — the AI replies in whatever language the customer writes
- **One inbox** — WhatsApp, Instagram, email, phone all in Dowaba
- **Secure** — Bearer token + sha256, IP whitelist, 2-step order confirmation, audit log
- **5-minute setup** — finish the wizard, paste the manifest URL into Dowaba, done

### Install
1. Install the module (`composer require dowaba/module-ai-connector` or zip)
2. `bin/magento setup:upgrade`
3. Admin → Dowaba AI → Setup & Settings → 5-step wizard
4. dowaba.com panel → Bundle Import → manifest URL + API key
5. Test from your WhatsApp Business line

### Compatibility
Magento Open Source & Adobe Commerce 2.4.4 – 2.4.7+ · PHP 8.1 / 8.2 / 8.3
