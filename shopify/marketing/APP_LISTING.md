## 5. Detailed Description (EN, 500+ words)

```markdown
# Turn conversations into Shopify orders.

DoWaba is an AI-powered conversational commerce platform that lets your Shopify customers **search products, get answers, compare options, and place orders — all from WhatsApp, Instagram DM, TikTok, Telegram, Messenger, web widget, or even phone calls.**

When a customer messages you on WhatsApp asking "Do you have iPhone 15 Pro?", DoWaba's AI queries your Shopify store in real-time, replies with stock + price, suggests alternatives, builds a draft order, gets customer confirmation, and creates a real Shopify order with `draftOrderComplete` — automatically.

No manual chat handling. No copy-pasting product info. No abandoned conversations.

## What it does for your Shopify store

🛒 **Real-time product search** — Customer asks "show me wireless headphones under $100" → AI runs Shopify products query → replies with matches + variants + stock.

⚖️ **Side-by-side comparison** — "What's the difference between iPhone 15 and 15 Pro?" → AI fetches both products + compares attributes in natural language.

📦 **Order from chat** — Customer says "I want 2 of these in size L" → AI creates Shopify draft order → shows summary (subtotal, shipping, tax, total) → asks "Confirm?" → on "Yes", completes draft into real order with status URL.

🛡️ **Safe by design** — Two-step order confirmation (no accidental orders). Read-only mode default; order creation requires explicit toggle.

🔐 **Bank-grade security** — OAuth 2.0 + HMAC-SHA256 webhook verification + encrypted token storage. Full GDPR compliance (customers/data_request, customers/redact, shop/redact webhooks).

📊 **Multi-channel inbox** — All conversations (WhatsApp, Instagram, TikTok, Telegram, Messenger, web widget, phone, email) in one panel.

🤖 **AI Voice Calls** — Each store gets its own SIP phone number. AI answers calls in natural Turkish/English/100+ languages, answers product questions, takes orders.

📅 **Built-in appointment system** — If you sell services + products, AI can book appointments synced across all channels.

🇹🇷 **Built for Turkey, scales globally** — Turkish UI default, English fully supported. Trendyol integration included for Turkish merchants.

## How to install

1. Click "Install app" → approve permissions (one-time)
2. In DoWaba panel: Sites → Integrations → "Shopify Store" → paste the manifest URL
3. 10 AI functions activate automatically
4. Connect WhatsApp Business / Instagram Business (Meta OAuth) — also from DoWaba panel
5. Customers can now chat with your store

## Requirements

- Shopify store on any plan (Basic / Shopify / Advanced)
- WhatsApp Business / Instagram Business account (for chat channels)
```

## 12. CASA Level 1 Questionnaire (read_customers için)

Shopify "Protected customer data access" başvurusu için form. Cevaplar:

- **Why do you need access to customer data?**
  > "DoWaba's `customer_lookup` AI function uses phone/email to identify returning customers from chat channels (WhatsApp, Instagram DM). This lets the AI personalize responses ('Hi Mehmet, your last order #1001 shipped yesterday') and reduce friction in chat-based ordering. Customer data is only displayed to the merchant in their authenticated DoWaba admin panel, never shared with third parties."

- **Data retention period:**
  > "Customer references (phone, email, name) cached for 24 hours. Order references kept for the chat conversation lifetime. Full deletion within 7 days of customer/data_request webhook, immediate within shop/redact."

- **Security measures:**
  > "All OAuth tokens encrypted at-rest with APP_KEY (Laravel encrypted cast). HTTPS only (TLS 1.3). HMAC-SHA256 webhook verification. Read-only by default; write scope (draft_orders) requires explicit per-site toggle. Full audit log of every API call with 30-day retention."
